## Notes from the build (the "As run" log)
- **Slice 1, 2026-11-04.** The scratch database `replay021` was restored again from the 29 Oct dump (the first one, used for the intent's measurement, had been dropped) and migrated. `replay_harness.py` replays C-01 (8,849 views) and C-03 (9,463) in about 25 s each after a 65 to 75 s set-up (similarity model and employer counts).
  - **The check as planned ("recomputed equals stored") holds for C-03 only** (97.6 % of keyword scores equal, the 31 differences all explained by the employer-boilerplate rule). **For C-01 the stored scores are older than its keyword list** (only 1,653 of 8,849 views carry the current profile version, and on those the replay is never lower but higher on 1,107): the keyword edits do not bump `profile_version` and the rescore runs in the background. So for C-01 the baseline is "today's rules replayed on today's config", the same config on both sides of every before/after; this is what the spec's measures need.
  - **The direct proof is job 33238:** replayed in memory from its saved text, it gets 32.7 (keyword 36.7, similarity 13.3, 0 of 37 key words, the same two hits and eight missing words as the host page, which showed 33). The 33 is therefore real, not an effect of an old stored score.
  - **C-03 is a poor test of the language gap** (its keywords are leftovers of a developer's CV import, and its English postings score lower than its French ones, the other way round). It stays as a second profile for the "no harm" criteria; the same-role control is judged on C-01 and reported for C-03. To confirm with the owner when verifying.
  - Not in the slice: the harness's glossary on/off switch (it reads `settings.GLOSSARY_ON` once slice 4 adds it) and the same-role control baseline (slice 6, with the role concepts of slice 2).
  - **Side finding for the owner (outside this cycle's scope):** keyword edits are not followed by a guaranteed full rescore of the inbox (only 1,653 of 8,849 C-01 views, 1,634 of 3,050 visible ones, carry the current version in the dump). A candidate can therefore see stored scores older than their keyword list. Slice 11's rescore refreshes them for the inbox; a cause and a fix would be a separate cycle (candidate: "keyword edits rescore the inbox and say when it finished").

## Order and dependencies
- 1 first: nothing is measured before the harness reproduces today's stored scores, otherwise a "before and after" proves nothing. It also keeps the scratch database that slices 2 and 6 to 9 use (Docker Desktop must be running).
- 2 before 3 (the owner reads what exists) and before 4 and 5 (they use the lookup). The owner's reading (3) can run while 4 and 5 are built; the tests are re-run after his corrections.
- 4 and 5 are independent of each other once 2 is done; 6 needs both.
- **6 is the decision point.** If it fails, the plan is changed with the owner (the spec's option: word-set matching for multi-word keywords) before 7 to 9; 7 to 9 do not depend on which way the fix goes, only on its numbers.
- 10 after 9; 11 only after 10 shows Live and the owner has a snapshot; 12 last.

## Rollback
- Slices 1 to 9 change nothing hosted: drop `replay021` (`docker exec saxobeat-db-1 psql -U saxobeat -d postgres -c "drop database replay021"`), revert or abandon the commits.
- After the push (10): scores do not change until the rescore, so the push alone is harmless; `GLOSSARY_ON` off restores today's scoring.
- After the rescore (11): switch `GLOSSARY_ON` off and run the rescore again, which rewrites every deterministic score as today's rules do (AI reviews are kept, as `rescore_all` keeps them). The Neon snapshot taken before is the last resort.

## Risks
- **Job 33238 may not reach 35 on this fix alone** (phrase keywords, see the spec): slice 6 is built to find out before anything else is built on it.
- **A rescore reads every posting's text** of every candidate from the host: a Neon read cost; slice 9 puts a number on it and slice 11 is the owner's call. Calibration (cycle 008) may need a new reading afterwards.
- **A wrong equivalent raises the wrong jobs:** the false-friend guard, the owner's reading (3) and the crossing examples (6, 10) are the three checks.
- **The French reference has little to compare with:** the CV is English, so the French text is mostly the keywords' French terms; the scale constant is a judgement made on the replay (written down with its reason), not a measured truth.
- **The replay uses a 6-day-old dump:** the numbers are an estimate of the host's, restated in `verify.md`.
- Other cycles share this working tree (`seed_employers.py` is modified by someone else): I stage only this cycle's paths.
