## Order and dependencies
- 1 first: everything reads from the service. 2 to 5 depend only on 1 and on nothing else in the list, but are done in this order because the job page (2, 3) is the case the owner raised and the inbox (4, 5) reuses its tests' helpers. Each slice starts with its failing tests.
- 6 after 2 to 5 (the strings are final only then). 7 after 6. 8 after 7 so the screenshots show the final strings. 9 after 8, and only when the owner agrees. 10 after the deploy.
- Nothing here touches the hosted database; the only irreversible step is the push (slice 9), and the owner does it.

## Rollback
- Slices 1 to 6: `git revert` of the cycle's commit (or not committing yet: `git restore` the cycle's own paths). No data was written, so there is nothing to undo in a database.
- After the deploy: Render's previous deploy (the owner's one click) or the revert commit pushed. The app works with and without the cycle's code on the same data (no migration).

## Risks
- **Shared working tree.** `seed_employers.py`, the `021` folder and possibly `locale/` hold other work: stage by path, and look at `git diff --stat locale` before slice 6. If another cycle's catalogue edits are in the files, commit only this cycle's hunks (`git add -p`) or ask the owner.

- **The chain of the real pair is an assumption** until slice 10: the host's page shows "first listed 28 Oct", which matches 31673's first seen, but only the read after the deploy proves the link.
