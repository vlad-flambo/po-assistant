# BCG (Boston Consulting Group): the source, the terms and the check (2026-11-03, step 2 slice 15)

## What it is
`careers.bcg.com` is a **Phenom** site, which the existing `phenom` adapter reads: `{"host": "careers.bcg.com", "site": "global/en", "company": "Boston Consulting Group", "countries": ["France"], "max_jobs": 60}`. Its public search (`/widgets`, 100 jobs a request, narrowed to France on the server) lists **44 jobs for France, 43 of them in Paris / Ile-de-France** by our city rules (one is in Madrid with the country France); each job's page carries a schema.org `JobPosting` with the full description. The company is written "Boston Consulting Group", as the two BCG jobs already in the catalogue through aggregators have it, so they merge instead of duplicating.

**The job in feedback #3 is in it:** "Lead/Principal AI Tech Architect – Paris | BCG Platinion" (job id 56128), with two other BCG Platinion roles (58983, 58984). The pasted link was a `utm_medium=phenom-feeds` address (the feed Phenom sends to LinkedIn); the adapter's own address for it is `https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/job/56128`.

## Terms and robots (criterion S2.1, read 2026-11-03, before the source exists)
| Page | Automated reading | Reuse |
|---|---|---|
| `bcg.com/about/terms-of-use` (the link in the careers site's footer) | **not addressed** (no word about robots, crawlers, scraping or data mining) | the content is protected from "unauthorized copying and dissemination"; permission to view, make "a single copy" and print it "only for your own lawful, personal, noncommercial use"; "You may not use the Site for any commercial purpose"; job postings are not named |
| `careers.bcg.com/robots.txt` | allows `/widgets` and the job pages (the adapter checks both and read them); it disallows only tracking, login, chat, cart, thank-you and widget-helper paths | |

Reading: nothing forbids automated reading; the reuse clause is the same posture as the other sites (a personal tool; the product-level review is deferred, PRD.md §2). The "no commercial purpose" sentence is worth the product-level review's attention if Saxobeat ever stops being a personal tool.
